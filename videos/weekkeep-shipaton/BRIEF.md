## Intent

Create the public Shipaton demo for Weekkeep: a calm, credible product launch
  film that begins with the parent's problem, proves the native one-minute weekly
  flow and branded local share artifact, and closes on the privacy, craft, and
  RevenueCat business boundary. The emotional promise is more important than
  build activity: one ordinary week becomes a memory a parent can keep or share
  without adopting another daily habit.
